The NORTHSTAR practicals

One messy clinical study dataset, four weeks, one question: what has to be true before this data is safe and useful for building an AI model?

Week 5

First contact

Look before you touch. What the dataset is for, where it came from, FAIR in four questions, and mapping three fields to a standard.

Week 8

Governance in practice

How these pages work

Each week's page follows the session from start to finish. The timings on each page are a guide to a two-hour session, not a clock. You do not need to install anything or write any code to complete it, and you can work through it with the person next to you. There are four kinds of box:

Read

Explanation. Read it, then move on.

Key idea

The one thing you need to understand before the next question. If a question seems to come out of nowhere, the answer is in the key idea box just above it.

Run

Code that has already been written, shown in R and in Python. You do not need to understand every symbol: read the plain-English line under the code, then press Run to see what it produces on the real data.

Your turn

A question for you. Type your answer into the box. Nothing you type is marked or submitted: these pages are your own knowledge base for the block, and the ideas in them are what the assessment draws on.

Boxes marked Going further are optional extras for anyone who finishes early or wants to see more.

Your work is saved in this browser, not online

Everything you type is saved as you go, on the computer you are using. Close the tab and come back on the same computer and browser, and it will still be there. It does not follow you to another computer, and university PCs may wipe it when you log out.

So at the end of every session, use Download my work at the bottom of the page. That gives you a Word file of your notes. A backup file lets you carry on on another computer.

The data

NORTHSTAR is a synthetic dataset: it was made up for teaching, and no real patient is in it. It looks like an export from REDCap (a common research data system) for a study run across three hospitals. You will find out more about it as the weeks go on, and working out what you have not been told is part of week 5. It is deliberately imperfect. Finding the problems is the point.
